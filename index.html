02 — Origin

Every system fails eventually. What matters is what you built into it first.

No résumé here. Only the part that explains how I work.

  1. 始

    Curiosity came before permission

    I took things apart before anyone showed me how they worked. Most of it broke. Breaking things taught me where the load actually sits. No tutorial hands that over.

  2. 稽

    Repetition before flourish

    The interesting part of any craft arrives late. Everything before it is the same motion, performed until it stops requiring attention. I stayed in that stretch longer than was comfortable.

  3. 乱

    Precision inside chaos

    Deadlines compress, requirements shift, someone is always waiting. Calm is not a mood I arrive with. It's the residue of having made the decision once already, on paper, before it was urgent.

  4. 構

    Then I built structure instead of output

    Anyone can finish one task. I got more interested in what removes the task entirely: the pipeline, the template, the model wired into the place where the work actually happens.

  5. 今

    Now

    I build with AI, automation, and plain engineering, and I write down how it works so the system outlives my attention. The record is in the Vault.
